The bull case for Daigneault hinges on OKC dramatically overperforming preseason projections while maintaining roster health and chemistry. The bear case is simpler: Coach of the Year almost never goes to a team expected to be elite. Voters gravitate toward surprise narratives—a coach taking a lottery team to the playoffs or a struggling franchise to 50 wins. With Daigneault and OKC already established as contenders, he'd need both sustained excellence AND a competitor to underperform or struggle with injuries. If any of the Celtics, Lakers, or Nuggets—likely other favorites—have coaching disruption or injury catastrophes, narrative space opens. The real driver isn't Thunder performance—it's whether another coach's underdog story becomes too compelling to ignore.
