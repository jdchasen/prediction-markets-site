### How would this market resolve if Iran destroys Kharg Island's facilities itself to prevent enemy use?

This presents ambiguity—if an insurgent group represents a competing claim to Iranian governance, it may still be considered "Iranian control" under a different faction, though market resolution would depend on whether this constitutes the current Iranian state losing control as intended by the question.
