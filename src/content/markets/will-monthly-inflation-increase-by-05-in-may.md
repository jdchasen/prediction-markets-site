## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is a 0.5% monthly print so significant when year-over-year inflation is already below 3%?

Monthly readings above 0.5% indicate annualized inflation above 6%, signaling acceleration even if year-over-year figures lag due to base effects—it's the forward momentum that matters for Fed policy.

### How much would an April CPI beat move this market?

A 0.45%+ monthly print in April (released May 14) would likely push YES odds to 60-70%, since it would suggest the May print faces upside risk from the same drivers.

### Could shelter costs alone push this over 0.5% if other categories cool?

Unlikely—shelter typically runs 0.3-0.4% monthly, so other categories (food, energy, goods) would need to add 0.1-0.2%+ to cross the threshold, requiring external shocks.
