The bull case for Reeves rests on Virginia Republicans' demonstrated willingness to nominate more conservative, non-establishment figures—particularly given internal divisions between business-aligned moderates and Trump-aligned grassroots activists. If establishment frontrunners stumble, face unexpected scandals, or withdraw for higher office, a consolidated conservative base could theoretically coalesce around Reeves as a true believer. However, this scenario requires multiple favorable dominoes falling simultaneously in a compressed timeframe.

Recent Virginia Republican nominees have been either sitting U.S. Representatives or wealthy self-funders; Reeves fits neither profile. The June 2026 expiry means nomination decisions will be largely finalized by late 2025, leaving minimal time for late surges.

Watch the Virginia GOP State Central Committee's decision on nomination rules (typically set by summer 2025) and early statements from potential candidates like House members Bob Good or Ben Cline.
