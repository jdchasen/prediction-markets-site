## Frequently Asked Questions

### What does "fully diluted valuation" mean for this market and why does it matter for the prediction?

FDV is calculated as token price × total token supply (including locked and unvested tokens), not just circulating supply. This matters because a project could hit $250M FDV with a low token price if supply is massive, making it easier to clear the threshold than a market cap–based prediction would require.

### If Ink launches on a DEX first rather than centralized exchanges, how does that affect the prediction outcome?

DEX-only launches typically result in lower initial volume and volatility, potentially making it harder to establish a clear FDV price discovery within the first 24 hours, though if liquidity pools are sufficiently capitalized, the probability remains high.
