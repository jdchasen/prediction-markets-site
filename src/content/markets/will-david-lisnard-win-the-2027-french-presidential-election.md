### What advantage does Lisnard's position as president of the Association of Mayors of France give him in a presidential campaign?

### How would Lisnard need to perform in Les Républicains' primary to have a realistic path to the presidency?

### Could a scenario where both Macron's successor and Le Pen stumble create an opening for Lisnard?
