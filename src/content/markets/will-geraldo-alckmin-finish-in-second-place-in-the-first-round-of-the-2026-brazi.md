The bull case requires several unlikely convergences: Alckmin's approval ratings would need to climb substantially from current levels (typically 20-30%), he would need to successfully position himself as the moderate alternative to both the far-left and far-right candidates, and the fragmented center-left coalition would need to coalesce around him rather than splinter across multiple candidates.
