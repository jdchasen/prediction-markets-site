## Market Analysis

The market values Macri's presidential comeback at less than 7%, reflecting skepticism that Argentina's former president can reverse his political fortunes after a disappointing 2015-2019 term and subsequent electoral defeats. This matters now because Argentina enters 2025 under Javier Milei's radical libertarian experiment, creating uncertainty about whether voters will reward continuity, seek a moderate alternative, or punish the right entirely by 2027.

The bull case rests on Macri's institutional strength: he controls Buenos Aires province through his Republican Proposal (PRO) party, maintains a donor network and business backing, and could benefit from Milei fatigue if the economic shock therapy fails. If inflation remains above 50% year-over-year through 2026, or if Milei's coalition fractures in legislative elections scheduled for October 2025, center-right voters might consolidate around Macri as a "known quantity" rather than risk further disruption. His moderate positioning also appeals to swing voters alienated by Milei's volatility. Argentina's primary elections (likely August 2027) and October general election create windows where he could clinch the nomination if he leads in polling by mid-2027.

The bear case is more compelling: Macri presided over rising unemployment and inflation (7-35% by 2019), and voters rejected him decisively in 2019 (48% to 40% loss against Alberto Fernández). Peronism, despite internal dysfunction, remains Argentina's largest political force and could reunite for 2027 if infighting subsides. Milei's current approval ratings (though volatile) suggest his coalition might hold enough seats to prevent Macri's comeback. Additionally, if Milei's shock therapy succeeds in stabilizing the peso and reducing inflation, the "anti-incumbent" vote fractures, reducing Macri's path.

Traders should monitor October 2025 midterms closely—if PRO gains ground or Milei's coalition collapses, Macri's odds could spike. Watch for primary polling data emerging in late 2026 and any criminal trials against Macri (judicial risks remain pending). The key inflection point is August 2027's primary; if he doesn't place first or second there, the market odds appear appropriately skeptical.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Could Macri's control of Buenos Aires province be leveraged to build a 2027 campaign machine?

Possibly, but provincial power hasn't translated to national victory for Macri before—Peronist provinces still hold 40% of the electorate, and Buenos Aires alone won't overcome a fragmented center-right vote if Milei retains 20-25% support.

### What role will Milei's coalition play in determining Macri's viability?

If Milei's libertarian bloc collapses before 2027, PRO and Macri could inherit moderate right-wing voters; if it stabilizes around 25-30% support, it splinters the center-right and makes Macri's path to a runoff much harder.

### Are there legal barriers preventing Macri from running in 2027?

While several judicial cases against Macri exist, Argentine law doesn't bar candidacy based on pending trials, though conviction or conviction-related appeals could theoretically emerge between now and 2027—a tail risk traders should monitor.
