## Market Analysis

The bull case rests on Shelton's longstanding relationship with Trump and her alignment with his unorthodox monetary views, particularly her past advocacy for gold standard considerations and criticism of Fed independence. Trump nominated her for a Fed governorship in 2020, signaling genuine interest in placing her in monetary leadership. If Trump prioritizes personal loyalty and heterodox economic thinking over mainstream credentials, Shelton becomes a serious contender. Her appointment would also satisfy hard-money conservatives who view the current Fed as too interventionist.

The bear case is far stronger: Shelton failed Senate confirmation for the lesser Fed governor position in 2020, losing 47-50 with two Republicans voting against her. Senate Republicans, even with a likely majority, have shown limited appetite for her controversial positions on monetary policy, including past statements questioning the need for a central bank and advocating return to gold-based currency. The financial establishment broadly opposes her, and Trump has other loyal candidates with better credentials like Kevin Warsh or Kevin Hassett who could satisfy his political goals without the confirmation battle.

Key catalysts include Trump's broader cabinet nomination patterns in early 2025, which will signal whether he's prioritizing confirmability or ideological purity. Powell's own statements about monetary policy through 2025 could influence Trump's calculus—if Powell maintains higher rates that Trump opposes, it might push him toward more unconventional choices. Watch for trial balloons floated to Republican senators, particularly Banking Committee members, around late 2025. Any public statements from Trump about Fed policy or Shelton specifically would move these odds significantly, as would Republican Senate leadership elections that could shift the confirmation environment.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why did Shelton's 2020 Fed governor nomination fail despite Republican Senate control?

Two Republican senators—Romney and Collins—joined Democrats in opposing her, citing concerns about her shifting positions on monetary policy and previous advocacy for pegging the dollar to gold. Her confirmation hearing revealed inconsistencies in her economic views that alienated even GOP members.

### When exactly would Trump need to announce his Fed chair nominee?

Powell's term expires May 15, 2026, but presidents typically announce Fed chair selections several months in advance to allow for vetting and Senate Banking Committee hearings. Expect nomination signals by late 2025 or early 2026 at the latest.

### Who are the more likely candidates if Shelton remains too controversial for Senate confirmation?

Kevin Warsh, a former Fed governor under Bush, and Kevin Hassett, Trump's former CEA chair, are frequently mentioned as having both Trump loyalty and Wall Street credibility that could secure easier Senate confirmation while still representing a hawkish shift from Powell.
