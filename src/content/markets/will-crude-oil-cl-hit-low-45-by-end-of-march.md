## Frequently Asked Questions

### What oil price level would need to be breached first to suggest $45 is becoming realistic?

A sustained break below $60 per barrel would be the first major warning sign, as this would indicate either significant demand destruction or a collapse in OPEC+ production discipline. Current technical support levels around $65-67 have held relatively firm throughout 2024.

### Has crude oil ever traded at $45 in recent history, and under what conditions?

Yes, WTI crude traded in the $40s during the COVID-19 pandemic collapse in spring 2020 and briefly in early 2021 during the recovery phase. It also spent time near these levels in 2016 following the shale boom oversupply situation, demonstrating that extraordinary circumstances are typically required.

### How would a $45 oil price impact U.S. shale production and potentially create a supply-side floor?

Most U.S. shale operators have breakeven costs between $40-55 per barrel, meaning $45 oil would trigger significant production cuts and rig count reductions within 3-6 months, naturally tightening supply and creating upward price pressure that would work against sustained sub-$45 levels.
