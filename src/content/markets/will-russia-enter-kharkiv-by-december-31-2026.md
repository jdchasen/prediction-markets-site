Conversely, the bear case—reflected in the current 92% NO probability—rests on substantial defensive advantages. Critically, any major escalation or shift toward Kharkiv would require Russia to either achieve breakthrough operations elsewhere first or accept substantially higher casualties. Political pressure for negotiated settlement (whether through 2025 U.S. political transitions or European fatigue) could freeze lines well before Russian forces reach the city.

Watch for two key catalysts: the evolution of front-line positions in Donetsk and Luhansk throughout 2025 will signal whether Russia maintains offensive momentum or becomes bogged down.
