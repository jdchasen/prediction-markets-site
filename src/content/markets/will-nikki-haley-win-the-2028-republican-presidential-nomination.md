## Market Analysis

The bull case rests on significant structural changes to the Republican electorate by 2028. If Trump declines to run or his influence wanes substantially—whether through age, legal issues, or a 2024 general election loss—the party could pivot toward electability-focused candidates. Haley maintains strong favorability among college-educated Republicans and suburban voters, demographics the GOP may need to recapture swing states. Her UN and gubernatorial experience, combined with substantial donor networks from her 2024 run, position her as a ready alternative if the party seeks to move beyond MAGA populism. A Trump loss in November 2024 could trigger immediate recriminations about electability that benefit her positioning for 2028.

The bear case is overwhelming and explains the sub-1% pricing. Haley's 2024 primary performance showed a hard ceiling with the current GOP base, struggling to win even moderate-leaning states. Her refusal to immediately endorse Trump, followed by her eventual endorsement, alienated both wings of the party. The Republican primary calendar heavily favors candidates with strong evangelical and rural support—areas where Haley consistently underperformed. More fundamentally, if Trump runs in 2028 (constitutionally barred only if he wins in 2024), he would dominate the field; if he doesn't run but endorses a successor like JD Vance or one of his children, that candidate inherits the MAGA infrastructure that has proven decisive in recent primaries.

Key catalysts include the November 2024 election results, which will determine whether Trumpism is validated or rejected by general election voters. The 2026 midterms provide another data point on voter appetite for Trump-aligned versus establishment candidates. Iowa caucus and New Hampshire primary dates in early 2028 will be critical, though they're not yet scheduled. Traders should monitor Trump's health and legal situations, potential candidate announcements beginning in 2026-2027, and whether Haley takes on a visible party role or maintains distance. Any major Trump family decision to run would effectively close this market.
