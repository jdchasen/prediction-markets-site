## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why did Katie Porter's 2024 Senate loss hurt her gubernatorial chances so significantly?

Losing a statewide race just two years before demonstrates weakness in fundraising, coalition-building, and voter appeal across California's diverse regions. Voters and donors typically hesitate to reinvest in candidates who recently failed to win statewide office.

### What advantage does Lieutenant Governor Eleni Kounalakis have over Porter in this race?

Kounalakis has been actively campaigning since 2023, building relationships with county party organizations and major donors while holding statewide office that provides name recognition and a built-in platform for media coverage.

### Could California's jungle primary system specifically disadvantage Porter compared to a traditional primary?

Yes, because she needs to finish top-two among all candidates regardless of party, her progressive lane could be crowded while a moderate Democrat consolidates establishment support, potentially leaving her squeezed out even with a dedicated base.
