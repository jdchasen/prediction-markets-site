The CONMEBOL qualifying matches in March and June 2025 will be critical indicators of whether tactical issues are being resolved.

Key catalysts include the final CONMEBOL qualifying window in September 2025, which will reveal Brazil's form heading into the tournament, and the World Cup draw in late 2025 that determines their path.
