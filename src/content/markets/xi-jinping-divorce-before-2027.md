### How would a Xi divorce become publicly known given Chinese censorship?

It would likely require either an official CCP announcement (extremely unlikely unless deemed regime-stabilizing), leaked foreign intelligence, or Xi's loss of power forcing the narrative public. The current market odds don't adequately price the information asymmetry.

### What's the actual track record of Chinese leaders divorcing while in power?

No modern Chinese paramount leader has divorced in office; Xi's predecessors maintained formal marriages regardless of personal arrangements. This historical precedent supports the bear case and helps explain the 1.6% floor.

### Could factional infighting force a divorce as a political weapon?

Theoretically yes, but Xi's 2024 power consolidation (purging rivals like Sun Zhengcai) made him less vulnerable to this tactic than predecessors, making the scenario less probable than the odds might suggest.
