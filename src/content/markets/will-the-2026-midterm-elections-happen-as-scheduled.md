## Market Analysis

The bull case for scheduled elections centers on institutional resilience and legal constraints. The Constitution mandates congressional terms ending January 3, 2027, creating an automatic deadline that requires elections. States control election administration under federalism principles, making any nationwide postponement legally and practically impossible without constitutional amendment. Historical precedent strongly supports this view—the U.S. has held elections during the Civil War, both World Wars, and the Great Depression without delay. Even the COVID-19 pandemic in 2020 resulted in procedural modifications but no postponement. The 50-state decentralized system makes coordinated disruption exceptionally difficult, and courts have consistently rejected attempts to alter election dates outside narrow, localized circumstances.

The bear case, though speculative, involves extreme scenarios like major military conflict, catastrophic infrastructure attacks, or constitutional crisis. Severe economic collapse or large-scale civil unrest could create pressure for emergency powers, though even these scenarios would more likely result in modified procedures rather than full postponement.

The first congressional primaries typically occur in March-May 2026, and any disruption attempts would need to materialize before those dates. Watch for emergency powers legislation or executive orders related to election administration, particularly in response to cybersecurity threats or international conflicts. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### What would legally be required to postpone federal elections beyond November 2026?

A constitutional amendment or unanimous state-level action would be necessary, as no federal law currently allows postponement. Congressional terms expire January 3, 2027 by constitutional mandate, creating an immovable deadline that effectively requires elections.

### Has the U.S. ever postponed a federal election due to emergency?

No federal election has ever been postponed nationwide. New York City delayed its primary by two weeks after 9/11, representing the most significant modern precedent, but general elections have proceeded through every crisis including the Civil War and pandemics.

### What scenarios would cause this market to drop below 80% probability?

A major cyber attack disabling election infrastructure across multiple states, credible threats of widespread violence coordinated for Election Day, or an actual constitutional crisis where competing authorities claim legitimacy could shift odds significantly downward in the months preceding November 2026.
