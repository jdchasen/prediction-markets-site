## Market Analysis

The bull case rests on political chaos scenarios: Netanyahu's ongoing corruption trial could result in a conviction forcing his exit, while simultaneous scandals or security failures could sideline Benny Gantz and Yair Lapid. Sa'ar has positioned himself as a right-wing alternative with government experience, and if Likud needed a replacement candidate who could maintain coalition unity without Netanyahu's baggage, Sa'ar could emerge as a compromise choice. His hawkish security credentials and previous ministerial positions give him legitimacy within the right-wing bloc. A scenario where early elections are called in 2025 following coalition collapse, combined with front-runners becoming unviable, could create an opening.

The bear case is overwhelming: Recent polling consistently shows Sa'ar's United Right party at 2-4 seats if it ran independently, far below the 30-35 seats needed to lead a government. Netanyahu maintains iron control over Likud despite legal troubles, and succession discussions within the party focus on figures like Yoav Gallant or Nir Barkat rather than Sa'ar. Gantz's National Unity party polls at 20+ seats, making him the clear alternative to Netanyahu. Sa'ar lacks a clear path to party leadership in either Likud (where he's seen as a past defector) or as an independent force. The next scheduled elections aren't until 2026, but even in early election scenarios, Sa'ar doesn't appear in polling as a likely Prime Minister candidate.

Key catalysts include any verdict in Netanyahu's trial (timing uncertain but potentially in 2025), coalition stability tests around the 2025 budget passage in March, and potential early election triggers from coalition defections. Traders should monitor Sa'ar's polling numbers if he rebuilds a separate party, watch for leadership challenges within Likud if Netanyahu exits, and track whether National Unity maintains its position as the primary alternative government. The October 2026 municipal elections could serve as a bellwether for party strength ahead of potential national elections later that year.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What happened when Sa'ar previously left Likud, and why does that matter for his current chances?

Sa'ar left Likud in December 2020 to form New Hope, winning only 6 seats in the 2021 election despite initial optimism, then merged with other parties before rejoining Likud in 2023. This history of defection and limited electoral success makes him an unlikely choice for Likud leadership and damages his credibility as an independent alternative.

### Could Sa'ar become Prime Minister without winning an election outright?

Yes, if Netanyahu resigned or became incapacitated and Likud held the most Knesset seats, the party could theoretically choose Sa'ar to form a government, though internal party dynamics make this extremely unlikely given his defection history and lack of factional support within Likud.
