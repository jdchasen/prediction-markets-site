## Market Analysis

The bear case is overwhelming: Cerrón has been in hiding since October 2023 to avoid a 3.5-year prison sentence for corruption during his tenure as Junín regional governor. Peruvian law prohibits candidates with pending prison sentences from running for office, creating an insurmountable legal barrier. His Peru Libre party, which brought Pedro Castillo to power in 2021, has seen its approval ratings crater below 5% following Castillo's removal and imprisonment in December 2022. The party's association with government instability, corruption allegations, and attempted constitutional violations has destroyed its electoral prospects. Even if Cerrón somehow resolved his legal issues, he would face first-round elections in April 2026 where polling consistently shows leftist candidates struggling to reach double digits amid public backlash against the Castillo era.

The bull case requires an extraordinary sequence of events: Cerrón would need his conviction overturned or pardoned, potentially through a sympathetic future government or judicial reversal. A dramatic shift in Peru's political landscape could theoretically occur if economic conditions deteriorate severely or if corruption scandals engulf all mainstream candidates, creating space for a Peru Libre resurgence. Historical precedent shows that Peruvian politics can experience rapid realignments—Castillo himself was polling in single digits months before his surprise 2021 victory. Additionally, if Boluarte's approval ratings (currently around 3-8%) continue to poison centrist and right-wing candidates by association, anti-establishment sentiment could create unexpected openings.

Key catalysts include any developments in Cerrón's legal proceedings, Peru Libre's performance in the January 2026 congressional candidate registration deadline, and polling data from late 2025 showing first-round preferences. Traders should monitor whether Cerrón surfaces from hiding and any constitutional court rulings on candidate eligibility standards. The official candidate registration period closing in January 2026 will definitively determine whether Peru Libre can even field Cerrón or must select an alternative.

### What happened to Peru Libre's electoral coalition after Pedro Castillo's presidency ended?
