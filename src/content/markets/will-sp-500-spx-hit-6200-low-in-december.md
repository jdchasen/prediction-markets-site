## Market Analysis

What matters now is whether the market can sustain earnings growth and maintain multiple expansion through 2026 without a major correction or recession derailing the rally.

A hard landing from rate cuts, trade wars (Trump tariffs post-January 2025), or a major geopolitical escalation could trigger a 15-25% correction that would make $6,200 unreachable. Additionally, if the Fed maintains rates above 4.5% through 2026 due to sticky inflation, equity multiples would likely contract, offsetting earnings gains.
