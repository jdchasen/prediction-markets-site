### Who are the likely frontrunners Miller would need to overcome in the 2026 LA mayoral race?

City Controller Kenneth Mejia and City Attorney Hydee Feldstein Soto are positioning as potential candidates, along with various city council members, though the field remains fluid as incumbent Karen Bass hasn't definitively ruled out seeking re-election.

### What fundraising threshold would Miller need to reach to be considered viable in Los Angeles?

Competitive LA mayoral candidates typically raise $5-8 million minimum, with frontrunners often exceeding $10 million given the expensive media market and city's geographic sprawl requiring substantial advertising and field operations.

### How does LA's primary system affect an underdog candidate's chances?

Los Angeles uses a top-two primary system where all candidates compete in March, with the leading two advancing to November regardless of party—this makes it extremely difficult for crowded-field candidates polling below 15% to survive the initial round.
