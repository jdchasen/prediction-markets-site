## Market Analysis

Miller has also positioned himself favorably on public safety issues at a time when crime concerns remain elevated among LA voters, potentially appealing to moderate Democrats who swung toward Rick Caruso in the 2022 mayoral runoff. His ability to consolidate endorsements from City Council members in key districts like the San Fernando Valley could prove decisive in a crowded primary field expected in March 2026.

The bear case questions whether Miller can overcome name recognition challenges against better-known candidates likely entering the race, including current City Council President Paul Krekorian and Controller Kenneth Mejia, who won citywide in 2022. Miller's relatively short tenure in city politics compared to establishment figures creates vulnerability, and any missteps on homelessness policy—LA's most visible crisis—could quickly erode support. The city's progressive activist base may also coalesce around a candidate further left than Miller, splitting the vote in unpredictable ways during the primary and potentially preventing him from reaching the June runoff.

Key catalysts include the candidate filing deadline in December 2025, when the full field becomes clear, and the March 2026 primary that will determine the top-two finalists. Traders should monitor upcoming City Council votes on homelessness encampment enforcement and housing development projects, as these issues consistently rank highest in LA voter surveys. Quarterly campaign finance disclosures through 2025 will signal institutional support, while any major endorsements from Congressmembers Karen Bass or previous mayoral candidates would substantially shift these odds.
