## Market Analysis

The bull case for his departure centers on Morocco's electoral calendar and deteriorating political capital. Parliamentary elections must occur by October 2026 at the latest, five years after the September 2021 vote that brought Akhannouch's National Rally of Independents (RNI) to power. His government has faced sustained criticism over rising living costs, earthquake recovery management following the September 2023 Al Haouz disaster, and water scarcity issues. Recent polling suggests declining approval ratings, and Morocco's fragmented political landscape makes coalition formation unpredictable. Even if the RNI performs well, the King could appoint a different prime minister from the winning coalition, as Moroccan constitutional practice grants the monarch considerable discretion in selecting the head of government. The timing almost guarantees a transition, as elections will likely occur in summer or fall 2026, with government formation taking several weeks afterward.

The bear case requires Akhannouch to both win the 2026 elections convincingly and retain the King's confidence for reappointment. If elections occur early in 2026 and the RNI dominates, he could theoretically remain in office through year-end. Morocco's economic growth has remained relatively stable at around 3%, and the government has pushed through infrastructure projects and administrative reforms that could translate into electoral support. The RNI's strong performance in the 2021 elections, where it secured 102 of 395 seats, demonstrated organizational capacity that could repeat. Morocco's monarchy typically values continuity in competent technocratic leadership, and Akhannouch's business background aligns with the regime's modernization agenda.

Key catalysts include any announcement of the election date, which must come at least 90 days before voting. Watch for spring 2026 political positioning, as parties typically intensify campaigning six months ahead. The formation talks following elections will be critical—Morocco's 2021 government took nearly two weeks to form after the September 8 election. Any major protests over economic conditions or government handling of the ongoing drought crisis could accelerate political turnover. The market essentially hinges on whether elections occur early enough in 2026 for a transition before December 31, making the official election date announcement the single most important catalyst.
