## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does a film need theatrical release to win Best Documentary Feature at the Oscars?

No, but it requires Academy voter visibility—either theatrical distribution, festival prominence, or a major streaming platform backing (like Netflix or National Geographic). Limited release and awards buzz can compensate for narrow theatrical reach.

### When does Oscar voting for documentaries actually close, and what's the timeline to watch?

Voting closes in late February 2026 (usually mid-month). The shortlist announcement in January is the critical inflection point; films on it gain credibility and voter attention, while those left off face steep odds even with high quality.

### Has a documentary critical of an authoritarian regime won Best Documentary Feature in the last decade?

Yes, "20 Days in Mariupol" in 2023, but it was exceptional—most documentaries addressing geopolitical conflict or authoritarianism have been nominated but not won, suggesting genre fatigue or voter preference for other documentary themes in most years.
