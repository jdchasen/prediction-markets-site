## Frequently Asked Questions

### What types of legislation would qualify as an "AI safety bill" for this market?

The market likely requires substantive federal legislation specifically addressing AI safety risks, such as mandatory testing for frontier models, safety standards for high-risk applications, or incident reporting requirements. Generic research funding bills or minor updates to existing laws probably wouldn't qualify unless they contain meaningful safety provisions.

### How do the 2026 midterm elections affect the probability of passage?

Midterm elections effectively create a hard deadline around mid-2026, as Congress typically becomes paralyzed 6-8 months before elections when members focus on campaigning. This compresses the viable legislative window to roughly 18 months starting January 2025, making early momentum in the 119th Congress critical.

### Could state-level AI safety laws impact federal legislation timeline?

Yes—if multiple states pass conflicting AI regulations in 2025, industry pressure for federal preemption could actually accelerate congressional action, similar to how state privacy laws eventually pushed federal privacy legislation forward. Conversely, comprehensive state laws might reduce urgency for federal intervention.
