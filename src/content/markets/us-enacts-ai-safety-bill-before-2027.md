## Frequently Asked Questions

### What specific types of legislation would count as an "AI safety bill" for market resolution?

The market likely requires federal legislation specifically addressing AI safety protocols, testing requirements, or liability frameworks—not merely AI-related provisions in broader bills. Minor amendments to existing laws may not qualify unless they establish substantive safety mechanisms.

### How do the 2024 election results affect the probability of AI safety legislation passing?

A unified government (single party controlling House, Senate, and Presidency) increases passage likelihood but doesn't guarantee it, as tech regulation doesn't break cleanly along party lines. Divided government requires bipartisan compromise, which could either produce moderate safety legislation or result in gridlock depending on which specific concerns gain traction.

### Could state-level AI regulations like California's attempted SB 1047 trigger federal preemptive legislation?

Yes—a patchwork of state regulations often prompts federal action as companies lobby for uniform national standards rather than navigating conflicting state requirements. If multiple states pass divergent AI safety laws in 2025-2026, industry pressure for federal preemption could accelerate congressional action.
