Thunberg's confrontational rhetoric and polarizing status in some political circles make her a risky choice. The G7 and G20 summits throughout 2025-2026 may produce climate commitments that elevate Thunberg's profile. Most critically, any major geopolitical peace breakthroughs—particularly regarding ongoing conflicts in Ukraine, Gaza, or emerging crises—would introduce competing candidates who would dramatically reduce Thunberg's already slim odds.
