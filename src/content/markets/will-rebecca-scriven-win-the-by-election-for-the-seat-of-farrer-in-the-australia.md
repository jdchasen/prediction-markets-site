## Market Analysis

The bear case for Scriven's chances rests on overwhelming structural factors: Farrer has been a National Party stronghold since 1984, with the Nationals' David Gillespie holding the seat with a commanding 20-point margin at the 2022 federal election. The electorate covers vast rural territory including Albury-Wodonga and the Riverina region, where conservative politics dominate and Labor has historically struggled to gain traction. By-elections typically favor opposition parties, but Labor would need a swing of unprecedented magnitude—over 10%—in hostile territory to flip this seat. The rural-urban divide in Australian politics has only deepened in recent cycles, with regional voters moving away from Labor on issues like climate policy, water management, and agricultural regulation.

The bull case, while remote, centers on potential unique by-election dynamics that could create an upset. If this by-election were triggered by scandal or controversial circumstances surrounding the incumbent's departure, voter sentiment could shift dramatically against the government. By-elections can produce protest votes that deviate from normal patterns, particularly if Labor runs a strong local candidate who can connect with farming communities on water rights, drought assistance, or rural healthcare—perennial concerns in the Riverina. A national swing against the Albanese government ahead of the 2025 federal election could amplify rather than diminish Labor's position if voters are punishing the Coalition in government scenarios, though this remains highly speculative.

Key factors to monitor include the timing and cause of any vacancy that triggers this by-election, as voluntary retirements produce different dynamics than forced departures. The selection of candidates by both major parties will be crucial—a well-known local National candidate would cement the status quo, while Labor would need an exceptional local figure to have any chance. Traders should watch polling from rural NSW seats and the broader national political environment as the May 2026 date approaches, though it remains uncertain whether this by-election will even occur by that date. Any major policy announcements affecting agricultural policy, water allocation in the Murray-Darling Basin, or rural infrastructure spending could marginally shift sentiment in this electorate.

### Has Labor ever won Farrer or come close in recent elections?

Labor has never won Farrer since the seat's creation, and their best modern performance was still a distant second place—the Nationals have maintained double-digit margins consistently, making this one of Australia's safest conservative seats.

### What would need to happen for these 0.3% odds to be justified rather than being effectively zero?
