## Market Analysis

The bull case for three cuts hinges on a meaningful economic slowdown materializing through 2025-2026. If monthly NFP reports consistently print below 100,000 jobs or unemployment rises above 5%, the Fed would face pressure to ease aggressively. A recession scenario where core PCE inflation drops to 2% or below while growth contracts would make three cuts plausible. Additionally, if financial stability concerns emerge—such as stress in commercial real estate or regional banking sectors—the Fed might prioritize growth over inflation targets. Watch the December 2024 and March 2025 FOMC meetings for any dovish pivots in the Summary of Economic Projections dot plot.

The bear case, which markets currently favor, assumes inflation remains above the Fed's 2% target throughout 2025, limiting cutting cycles. If core CPI continues printing at 3-4% annually or wage growth measured by Average Hourly Earnings stays elevated above 4%, the Fed will likely hold rates steady or cut minimally. The current pricing suggests traders expect at most one or two cuts in 2026, not three. Strong consumer spending, sustained labor market tightness (unemployment below 4.5%), or reacceleration in shelter costs would all support this view.

Key catalysts include the January 2025 CPI report (due mid-February 2025) which will set the tone for inflation trajectory, and each quarterly FOMC meeting in 2025 where officials update their rate projections. The February and March 2025 NFP reports will be critical for assessing labor market momentum. Traders should monitor the Fed's preferred core PCE gauge released monthly, typically the last week of each month. Any movement in this market will likely follow sustained trends across multiple data releases rather than single prints, requiring patience and attention to the Fed's December 2025 meeting where 2026 guidance becomes concrete.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many rate cuts would the Fed need to deliver in 2025 to make three 2026 cuts more likely?

If the Fed cuts four or more times in 2025, it would suggest a significant economic deterioration that could necessitate continued easing into 2026. However, aggressive 2025 cuts might also exhaust the cutting cycle, making further 2026 cuts less necessary.

### What unemployment rate would historically justify three rate cuts in a single year?

Three cuts typically occur when unemployment is rising rapidly, generally increasing by 0.5-1 percentage point or more year-over-year, or when it crosses above 5-5.5% with continued deterioration expected.

### Does this market resolve YES if the Fed cuts rates three or more times, or exactly three times?

The market requires at least three separate 25-basis-point equivalent cuts during 2026, so four or five cuts would also resolve YES, though current economic projections make even three cuts highly unlikely.
