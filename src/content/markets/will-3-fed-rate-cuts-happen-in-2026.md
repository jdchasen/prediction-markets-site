## Frequently Asked Questions

### What qualifies as a "rate cut" for this market's resolution?

This resolves on the federal funds target rate being reduced three separate times during 2026 by any amount, whether 25bp or 50bp cuts. Emergency inter-meeting cuts would count toward the three-cut threshold.

### Could the Fed cut three times even if inflation stays above 2%?

Yes, if unemployment spikes rapidly or financial conditions tighten severely, the Fed could prioritize the employment mandate over inflation, though current odds suggest traders view this scenario as unlikely given recent Fed communications emphasizing patience.

### How would geopolitical events or fiscal policy changes affect this market's probability?

Major disruptions like oil supply shocks would likely reduce cut probability by stoking inflation, while a fiscal crisis or government funding squeeze could increase it by threatening growth, though the Fed typically resists political pressure to maintain credibility.
