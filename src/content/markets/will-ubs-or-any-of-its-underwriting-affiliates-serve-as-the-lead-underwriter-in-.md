## Market Analysis

The bear case for UBS centers on SpaceX CEO Elon Musk's repeated statements that the company will remain private until Mars missions reach a regular cadence, which he's suggested won't happen until the late 2020s at earliest. Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley have dominated recent high-profile tech IPOs and maintain deeper relationships with Musk through Tesla's capital markets activities. Morgan Stanley specifically led Tesla's 2010 IPO and numerous follow-on offerings, creating path dependency for SpaceX if it does go public. Additionally, Bank of America has strengthened its position in aerospace and defense banking, while JPMorgan has aggressively pursued SpaceX's private financing rounds, positioning these banks ahead of UBS in the pecking order.

The bull case relies on UBS's growing ambitions in U.S. tech banking following its Credit Suisse acquisition in 2023, which brought additional dealmaking talent and client relationships. If SpaceX's Starship program achieves full operational status ahead of schedule—NASA's Artemis III moon landing is currently targeted for 2026—pressure from investors and employees holding illiquid equity could force an earlier IPO than Musk prefers. UBS could win the mandate by offering superior valuation, distribution capabilities in Europe and Asia where SpaceX may want investor diversification, or through relationships with SpaceX's institutional investors who might influence the selection process.

Key catalysts include Starship's orbital refueling demonstrations expected throughout 2025, which are critical for NASA's moon program and Mars feasibility. Any public comments from Musk about IPO timing would dramatically move this market, as would announcements of major investment banking mandates won by UBS in the aerospace sector that signal relationship-building. SpaceX's private funding rounds—the company raised capital at a $180 billion valuation in late 2023—offer clues about which banks are closest to the company. Traders should monitor whether SpaceX begins pre-IPO preparation activities like financial audits under public company standards or SEC engagement, though the current low odds appropriately reflect that these developments remain speculative through 2027.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Has SpaceX given any indication it plans to go public before the 2027 market expiry date?

No. Elon Musk has consistently stated SpaceX will remain private until Mars colonization missions are regular and predictable, a milestone he doesn't expect until the late 2020s or 2030s, well beyond this market's timeframe.

### Which investment banks are currently closest to SpaceX based on recent financing activity?

Morgan Stanley has maintained the strongest relationship through Tesla work and previous SpaceX transactions, while Bank of America and JPMorgan have participated in recent private funding rounds at valuations exceeding $150 billion, positioning them ahead of UBS.

### Could SpaceX be forced into an IPO earlier than Musk wants due to employee liquidity needs?

SpaceX has managed employee liquidity through periodic tender offers and secondary sales that allow staff to sell shares privately, reducing pressure for a public listing, though a major valuation decline or funding crunch could theoretically change this calculation.
