## Market Analysis

The bull case rests on TPP's explosive growth trajectory: Ko Wen-je's party captured 8% of the 2024 presidential vote despite being founded in 2022, and younger urban voters continue to migrate toward the reform-minded party as an alternative to the KMT-DPP duopoly. If TPP can convert its presidential coalition into local organization over the next 18 months, and if either the KMT or DPP fractures over internal leadership disputes or policy failures, the party could theoretically capture significant county-level representation. Ko's current imprisonment on corruption charges has created a narrative vacuum that could be filled by stronger local candidates, and spring 2025 will reveal whether his legal troubles translate to actual vote losses.

The bear case is more compelling: winning the *most* heads of local government requires dominating 13 of Taiwan's 22 counties and cities—a nearly impossible task for a party with minimal grassroots infrastructure. The KMT currently controls most major counties and has deep local machine politics; the DPP holds key urban centers like Taipei and Kaohsiung. TPP would need to crack into areas like Taichung, Changhua, and Yunlin simultaneously, displacing entrenched politicians with 20+ years of local networks. Ko's ongoing trial conclusion (expected mid-2025) will heavily influence candidate recruitment quality and donor confidence, while any major policy shift by the Lai administration could consolidate DPP support among swing voters.

Watch for three specific catalysts: Ko's court verdict timing in spring-summer 2025, which will determine whether TPP can attract A-list local candidates; July 2025's expected party primary schedule announcements from the KMT and DPP, which will reveal competitive intensity and potential defections; and autumn 2025 polling on local races, which will show whether TPP's presidential voters actually translate to willingness to vote for unknown county magistrates. If Ko is convicted and sentenced to prison time, odds should compress to zero. If he's acquitted and TPP announces strong candidates in 5+ major counties by September 2025, this market could see movement to 5-8%.
