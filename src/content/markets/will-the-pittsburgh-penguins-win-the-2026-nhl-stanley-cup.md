Their prospect pool remains thin after years of win-now trades, meaning reinforcements aren't arriving from within. Tristan Jarry's inconsistent goaltending has been a persistent problem, and the team's defensive structure allows too many high-danger chances. Most critically, the core's age makes them vulnerable to injury at the worst times—Crosby has missed significant games in recent seasons.
