## Frequently Asked Questions

### Has Ratinho held any significant political office before a potential 2026 presidential run?

Ratinho served as mayor of Curitiba from 1986-1988 and briefly as a federal deputy, but his political career has been limited compared to his four-decade television presence. His lack of recent political experience represents a significant barrier to mounting a credible national campaign.

### What would need to happen for these odds to move substantially higher?

Ratinho would need to formally declare candidacy with visible campaign infrastructure, secure major party coalition support beyond Podemos, and register at least 5-10% in credible national polling. Without these concrete developments by Q2 2025, the probability would likely remain in single digits.

### How does Brazil's two-round system affect outsider candidates like Ratinho?

Brazil requires a majority to win outright, otherwise the top two candidates advance to a runoff. This structure historically consolidates support around established politicians with broad coalitions, making it exceptionally difficult for outsiders without major party backing to reach the second round against frontrunners like Lula or leading opposition candidates.
