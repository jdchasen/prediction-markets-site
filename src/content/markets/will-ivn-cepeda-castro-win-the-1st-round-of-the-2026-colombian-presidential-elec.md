The bull case rests on Cepeda's consistent polling dominance within the left-wing coalition, his organizational infrastructure as a sitting senator and longtime progressive activist, and the fragmentation of centrist and right-wing opposition that could allow him to finish in the top two even with a plurality rather than majority.
