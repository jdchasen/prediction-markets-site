## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can Republicans pass the SAVE Act through budget reconciliation to avoid a filibuster?

Reconciliation is limited to provisions with direct budgetary effects, and voter registration requirements are primarily policy changes. The Senate parliamentarian would likely rule most SAVE Act provisions out of order for reconciliation unless they're rewritten with clear fiscal components.

### What specific proof of citizenship documents would the SAVE Act require that current law doesn't?

The SAVE Act would mandate documentary proof like passports, birth certificates, or naturalization papers at registration, whereas current federal law only requires attestation of citizenship under penalty of perjury. This represents a significant departure from the National Voter Registration Act's framework established in 1993.

### Why didn't the SAVE Act pass when Republicans controlled both chambers previously?

The SAVE Act as currently written was only introduced in 2024, and previous Republican trifectas (2017-2019) prioritized tax reform and healthcare over voting legislation. Even then, the narrower Senate margins made controversial voting bills difficult without Democratic cooperation given filibuster rules.
