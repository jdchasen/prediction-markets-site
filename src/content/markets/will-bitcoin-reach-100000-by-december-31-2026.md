### How does the 2024 halving timeline affect Bitcoin's chances of reaching $100k by end of 2026?

### What Bitcoin price level would miners need to remain profitable after the halving, and why does this matter?

Most industrial miners break even between $25,000-$40,000 post-halving depending on energy costs and efficiency. If prices fall below these thresholds for extended periods, forced selling from miners could create downward pressure preventing the $100k target.

### How much of Bitcoin's supply would need to remain off exchanges to support a $100k price target?
