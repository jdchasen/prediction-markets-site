### How has Italy historically performed in Eurovision televoting versus jury voting?

Italy typically performs stronger with juries than televoters, as their sophisticated entries appeal to music professionals. The 2021 Måneskin victory was exceptional in capturing both, but most Italian entries score higher with juries than public votes.

### Why does the market categorize Eurovision as "politics" when it's a song contest?

Eurovision voting patterns consistently reflect geopolitical alliances, diaspora populations, and regional bloc voting, making it as much a political exercise as a musical competition. Neighboring countries and cultural allies regularly exchange high points regardless of song quality.

### What advantage does the Sanremo Festival give Italy compared to other countries' selection methods?

Sanremo is Italy's premier music event with 70+ years of history, attracting top-tier artists and massive domestic viewership. This ensures Italy's Eurovision entry has already proven commercial appeal and performance polish, unlike internal selections or smaller national finals in other countries.
