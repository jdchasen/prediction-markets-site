### How is FDV calculated for this market and does it include locked tokens?

FDV multiplies the token launch price by total maximum supply including all locked, vested, and unvested tokens. Even if only 5% circulates initially, the calculation uses 100% of supply to determine if the threshold is met.

### What timeframe counts as "one day after launch" for settlement purposes?

The market resolves based on FDV exactly 24 hours after Variational's official token becomes tradeable on any public exchange or DEX. Peak intraday FDV during that 24-hour window determines the outcome, not closing price.
