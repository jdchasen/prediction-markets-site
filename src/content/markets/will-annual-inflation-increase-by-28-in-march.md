## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is 2.8% the specific threshold rather than 2.5% or 3.0%?

2.8% sits meaningfully above the Fed's 2% target but below historical baseline inflation, making it a key decision point for whether the Fed resumes rate hikes—traders use it as a proxy for "inflation uncontrolled enough to force tighter policy."

### If inflation reads 2.75% in February, does the market settle YES?

No—the resolution requires inflation to be ≥2.8%, so 2.75% would result in a NO payout; this tight threshold means the outcome could hinge on decimal-point rounding across multiple months' data.

### How much does the March PCE print matter versus the CPI reading?

The March PCE (released mid-April) arrives just before expiry, making it the final authoritative inflation snapshot; CPI readings in January and February are leading indicators that traders use to adjust positions before that final PCE release.
