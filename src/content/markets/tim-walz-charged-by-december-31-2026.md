## Frequently Asked Questions

### Could charges related to 2020 riot management still be filed given that over four years have passed without prosecution?

Yes—statutes of limitations for state felonies in Minnesota range from 3-9 years depending on severity, and civil litigation discoveries or political shifts could still trigger criminal referrals even now, though prosecutorial passivity this long suggests low appetite.

### How would Walz's vice presidency affect the likelihood he faces charges before 2027?

A sitting vice president being charged would face extraordinary DOJ institutional resistance and would appear starkly political, making prosecution highly unlikely even if underlying conduct was questionable—prosecutors typically avoid such cases absent overwhelming evidence of conventional crimes.

### What specific evidence would prosecutors need to move forward, given policy decisions are generally not prosecutable?

They would need proof of concrete criminal acts like fraud (misallocating pandemic funds), abuse of power (orders provably exceeding legal authority), or neglect of duty causing direct harm—not mere disagreement with his riot response strategy or emergency declarations.
