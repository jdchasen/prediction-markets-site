## Frequently Asked Questions

### What annual return rate does $500,000 Bitcoin by end of 2026 require from current levels?

From $100,000, it requires approximately 125% annualized returns over two years, far exceeding Bitcoin's historical average annual returns of 40-60% even during bull cycles. This would represent the steepest sustained rally in Bitcoin's history outside its sub-$10,000 days.

### Has Bitcoin ever achieved a 5x gain in a 24-month period at its current market maturity?

No—the last comparable percentage move occurred in 2019-2021 (from $7,000 to $69,000), but that started from a lower base and took 28 months. Since crossing $20,000, Bitcoin's largest two-year gain has been approximately 3.5x, making 5x historically unprecedented at this scale.

### What would need to happen to exchange reserves to support a $500,000 price target by 2026?

Exchange reserves would likely need to drop below 2 million BTC (from current ~2.3 million) while simultaneously seeing sustained institutional inflows exceeding $50 billion quarterly through spot ETFs. Current ETF flows of $10-20 billion quarterly are insufficient to drive this magnitude of price appreciation in the required timeframe.
