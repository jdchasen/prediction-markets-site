The 2025-26 season begins just months after promotion, with players needing adaptation time to Premier League intensity. Injuries to key players like Dan Neil or Jobe Rodon would devastate their thin squad. Additionally, Manchester City and Arsenal have shown they'll strengthen aggressively, meaning the ceiling for top-three keeps rising even as Sunderland works to improve.

A strong start with three wins in the first five games would incrementally shift odds upward, though structural disadvantages will likely keep this market sub-5% throughout the season. Watch for any major injury setbacks in pre-season, managerial changes, or unexpected departures of key Championship-era players—any of these would make a top-three finish even more remote.
