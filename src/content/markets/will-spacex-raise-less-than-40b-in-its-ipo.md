## Market Analysis

The bull case for SpaceX exceeding $40B rests on its dominant market position in commercial launch services, recurring Starshield government contracts, and Starlink's trajectory toward profitability with over 8 million subscribers generating ~$5B annual revenue run-rate. Morgan Stanley and other analysts have valued SpaceX at $180-210B based on Starlink's long-term satellite internet TAM alone, plus the core launch business growing at 30%+ annually. The company's recent funding rounds in 2023 valued it at $180B, and no material deterioration in business fundamentals has occurred since. A $40B IPO would represent an 80% discount to recent private valuations, an implausible markdown unless the company faces bankruptcy or regulatory catastrophe.

The bear case hinges on execution risks with Starlink profitability—the unit has faced higher capital expenditure than initially projected and competition from Amazon's Project Kuiper and OneWeb is intensifying. Additionally, government revenue concentration risk exists: if SpaceX loses Starshield contracts or faces regulatory restrictions on Starlink exports, revenue visibility drops sharply. Terrestrial 5G deployment has also accelerated, creating uncertainty around Starlink's rural broadband TAM. A severe recession in 2024-2025 could also compress tech multiples broadly, potentially affecting IPO pricing.

Watch for three catalysts: any Starlink subscriber slowdown or churn data in quarterly updates, changes to U.S. export restrictions on satellite broadband technology, and shifts in defense spending priorities under new administrations. The 2.8% odds suggest the market is pricing in ~99% confidence SpaceX exceeds $40B, which leaves limited room for downside surprises. Most traders should monitor whether Starlink's path to profitability remains credible and whether geopolitical tensions alter government demand for launch services.

### What would trigger a sub-$40B IPO valuation for SpaceX?

### If SpaceX IPOs in 2025, what revenue would justify a $40B valuation?
