## Market Analysis

The market has priced Quintero at essentially 0.2%, suggesting traders view his path to first-round victory as nearly impossible despite his prominence as Medellín's former mayor and current political figure. This valuation matters because Quintero remains moderately visible in Colombian politics, making such extreme pessimism worth interrogating—either the market is correctly identifying structural barriers to his candidacy or significantly underweighting his upside potential.

The bull case rests on Quintero's demonstrated ability to build urban governance credentials and his appeal to younger, progressive voters in major cities. As a former Medellín mayor credited with innovation in city management, he has a tangible track record that distinguishes him from many Colombian politicians. If he successfully consolidates the left-wing or centrist vote ahead of the March 2026 primary cycle, and if major frontrunners fragment or face scandals, Quintero could emerge as a consolidation candidate. Additionally, Colombian presidential dynamics are volatile—Gustavo Petro's unexpected 2022 victory demonstrated that outsider or previously underestimated candidates can surge. A strong primary showing in January-February 2026 would be the key inflection point for reassessing this market.

The bear case is more straightforward: Quintero faces established rivals with deeper national machines, superior funding networks, and higher name recognition outside Medellín. Major coalitions (likely including continuity candidates from Petro's government or opposition blocs) will coalesce around stronger frontrunners months before the May election. Colombian first-round structures typically require 50% plus one vote to avoid a runoff, or force a runoff if no candidate reaches this threshold—Quintero would need extraordinary momentum to crack even 25-30% in a crowded field. His lack of legislative power base and limited national infrastructure make early-stage coalition-building difficult. Watch for his formal candidacy announcement (expected late 2025 or early 2026) and polling releases through Q1 2026; if he remains below 5-8% in major polls by March, the 0.2% odds will likely hold.
