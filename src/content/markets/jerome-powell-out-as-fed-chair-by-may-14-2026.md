The bull case for Powell remaining relies on the Federal Reserve Act's statutory protections, bipartisan congressional support for Fed independence, and Powell's own demonstrated resilience through previous political storms. Markets and policymakers would react negatively to any removal attempt, creating economic costs that could deter even a determined administration.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can President Trump legally fire Jerome Powell before his term ends in May 2026?

Legal consensus holds that the President cannot remove a Fed Chair without demonstrating "for cause" reasons like misconduct or incapacity, not mere policy disagreements. Any removal attempt would immediately trigger federal court challenges with uncertain outcomes.

### What happens to monetary policy if Powell leaves early?

The Fed Vice Chair would assume leadership temporarily, but the vacancy would create uncertainty around rate decisions and potentially weaken the dollar. Senate confirmation of a replacement typically takes 2-4 months, prolonging instability.

### Has a sitting Fed Chair ever been forced out before completing their term?

No Fed Chair has been involuntarily removed from office in the institution's history since 1913. Several have chosen not to seek reappointment, but none have been fired or pressured to resign mid-term.
