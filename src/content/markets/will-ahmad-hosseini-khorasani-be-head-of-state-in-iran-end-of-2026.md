No credible reporting suggests Khorasani is positioned as a leading successor candidate. Khamenei has actively cultivated institutional loyalty and shown no signs of imminent incapacity; the Assembly of Experts historically chooses from a narrow roster of figures long groomed for the role. The window for succession by end-2026 remains extremely compressed. Even major regional conflict typically strengthens rather than destabilizes an incumbent supreme leader's grip on power.

Critical catalysts to monitor include any public announcement regarding Khamenei's health, statements from Assembly of Experts members about succession planning, and reactions to Iran's response to Israeli actions or U.S. negotiations. Watch for shifts in coverage by Iranian state media about potential successors, though official sources rarely signal this directly. Most likely, this market remains a tail-risk hedge against black-swan regime change scenarios rather than a reflection of genuine political momentum.
