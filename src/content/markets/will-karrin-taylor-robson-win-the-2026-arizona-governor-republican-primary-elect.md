The bull case for Robson hinges on potential fragmentation of the Trump-endorsed field and genuine dissatisfaction with Lake's 2022 general election loss to Democrat Katie Hobbs. Lake remains the presumed front-runner despite her general election loss, and other Trump-aligned candidates like gubernatorial candidates from the MAGA wing will likely enter the race. If Robson can't raise substantial sums by summer 2025, the market odds may drift even lower. Traders should watch for any statements from sitting Arizona Republican elected officials signaling support for her or distancing from Lake—such dynamics could shift primary dynamics meaningfully, though current structural headwinds make her path extremely narrow.
