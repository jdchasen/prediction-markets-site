### Why would the Fed pause in January if it's cutting in March—what economic event could trigger this reversal?

This is structurally improbable given the Fed's preference for data-driven gradual moves and advance signaling.

### Does this market expire before the April FOMC decision actually happens?

### If inflation falls sharply in February, would that make this sequence more likely?

A sharp decline could theoretically make a March cut plausible, but it wouldn't explain the January pause—the Fed would likely signal a March cut in advance or cut in January itself rather than pause first. Sharp disinflation typically prompts coordinated action, not the stop-start pattern this market requires.
