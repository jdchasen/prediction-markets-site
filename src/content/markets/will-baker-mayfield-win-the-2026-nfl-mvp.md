### How does Baker Mayfield's age affect his 2026 MVP candidacy?

At 31 during the 2026 season, Mayfield would be in the optimal age window where quarterbacks historically win MVP (typically ages 27-33), combining experience with physical prime. This timeline actually works in his favor compared to younger developmental quarterbacks.

### What statistical benchmarks would Mayfield need to reach for serious MVP consideration in 2026?
