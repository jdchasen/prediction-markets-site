## Market Analysis

The market is pricing in an overwhelming probability that the Bank of Israel will hold rates steady at its March 2026 decision, reflecting expectations of monetary policy stability despite ongoing economic pressures. This matters because Israel's central bank decisions directly influence shekel strength, bond yields, and inflation expectations—key factors for both domestic investors and those with exposure to Middle Eastern markets.

The bull case for a hold rests on several foundations: if inflation remains anchored near the BoI's 2% target through early 2026, the central bank has little incentive to move, especially given the geopolitical uncertainties that have characterized the region. The BoI has historically moved cautiously between decisions, preferring to observe multiple data points before shifting policy. Additionally, if global growth remains modest and the Federal Reserve maintains higher-for-longer rates (which would pressure shekel weakness if the BoI cut), a hold becomes the prudent choice. The bear case centers on potential inflationary shocks—another escalation in regional tensions, energy price spikes, or unexpected wage pressure could force the BoI's hand toward a rate increase before March. If core CPI readings from late 2025 and early 2026 consistently exceed 2.5%, market expectations will shift sharply toward tightening.

Watch for Israel's CPI releases (typically mid-month), particularly the January 2026 and February 2026 prints, which will be the freshest data the BoI considers at its March decision. FOMC decisions in January and March 2026 matter substantially—if the Fed signals a more dovish stance or cuts aggressively, the BoI may feel pressure to follow, while Fed hawkishness could support a hold. The BoI's own forward guidance and any emergency security-related fiscal spending announcements could also quickly reprice this market. Traders should monitor shekel weakness against the dollar as an indirect signal of rate expectations; significant depreciation might force the central bank to defend currency stability through a surprise hike.

### How does Fed policy in early 2026 mechanically affect this market?

### Could geopolitical escalation alone trigger a rate move by March?
