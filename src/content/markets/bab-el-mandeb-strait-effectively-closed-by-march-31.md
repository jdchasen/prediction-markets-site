Current geopolitical conditions make this outcome plausible but far from consensus.

The bull case rests on Houthi escalation. Commercial traffic has continued despite Houthi attacks, with only modest rerouting. Shipping insurance and security protocols have adjusted rather than capitulated. Unless Houthi capabilities leap dramatically or political will to maintain open transit collapses, the status quo of "contested but passable" likely persists through March 2025. Historical precedent suggests even significant piracy and attacks rarely achieve true functional closure of major straits.
