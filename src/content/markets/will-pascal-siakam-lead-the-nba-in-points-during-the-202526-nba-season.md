## Market Analysis

The market is pricing Siakam at a 0.9% probability of leading the NBA in scoring during 2025–26, reflecting widespread skepticism about whether he can surpass perennial scoring leaders like Luka Dončić, Kevin Durant, Stephen Curry, and Jayson Tatum. This extremely low odds level suggests the market sees structural barriers to Siakam winning the scoring title rather than viewing it as a near-impossible fluke outcome.

The bull case relies on two interconnected factors: increased offensive usage and sustained health. If the Toronto Raptors commit to running their offense through Siakam as the primary ball-handler—a role shift that could happen if the team pivots away from guard-centric play—his scoring volume could spike significantly. Siakam's career high in scoring average is 23.6 PPG (2019–20), and he's capable of 24+ PPG seasons, particularly if injuries to Raptors guards elevate his responsibility. The bear case is substantially more compelling and explains the 0.9% odds. Siakam has never led the league in scoring—he's peaked at 23.6 PPG in a weak scoring year—and he faces entrenched competition from multiple elite scorers in their primes. Dončić, Durant, Tatum, and Curry have all averaged 25+ PPG in recent seasons, and Siakam would need to average roughly 28–30 PPG to secure the title, a threshold he's never approached. The Raptors historically distribute scoring responsibility across multiple players, limiting individual volume. Injury history is also relevant: Siakam has missed games due to shoulder and hand injuries, which could disrupt his ability to sustain elite-level scoring over a full season.

Traders should monitor the 2025 offseason carefully for three catalysts: (1) potential Raptors roster trades or free-agency moves that would clarify Siakam's offensive role, (2) coaching staff decisions regarding offensive structure, and (3) early-season performance trends through November 2025, which would signal whether a surprising scoring surge is materializing. If Siakam isn't averaging 26+ PPG by the All-Star break in February 2026, the odds should compress further toward zero.
