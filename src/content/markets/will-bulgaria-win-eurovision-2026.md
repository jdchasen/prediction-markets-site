## Frequently Asked Questions

### Has Bulgaria ever won Eurovision before, and what's their recent track record?

Bulgaria has never won Eurovision in its participation history. Since returning to the contest in 2016, the country has shown inconsistent results with one runner-up finish (2017) but multiple years of non-qualification and voluntary withdrawals.

### What's the main risk that Bulgaria doesn't even compete in 2026?

Bulgarian National Television has repeatedly withdrawn from Eurovision due to funding constraints and disputes with contest organizers, including skipping 2019 and 2024. Financial pressures on the public broadcaster make participation uncertain until officially confirmed.

### Which countries would Bulgaria need to beat to actually win Eurovision?

Bulgaria would need to outperform traditional powerhouses like Sweden, Italy, France, and Ukraine, plus overcome disadvantages from limited voting bloc support compared to Nordic, Balkan, and Eastern European alliances that historically exchange points.
