## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is Kennealy's probability so low despite his Lt. Governor experience?

Massachusetts is a heavily Democratic state where the Republican nominee faces a structural disadvantage in the general election, reducing the value of winning the primary; additionally, his association with the moderate Baker administration may alienate primary voters in an increasingly populist GOP landscape.

### What would need to happen for Kennealy's odds to materially improve before the 2026 primary?

A fractured Republican field with multiple conservative candidates splitting votes, a significant economic downturn in Massachusetts that boosts Republican messaging, or a Democratic nominee vulnerable enough that a Republican primary win seems strategically valuable.

### How does the Massachusetts party convention system affect Kennealy's primary odds?

Massachusetts uses a convention-to-primary system where candidates must clear a 15% threshold at the state party convention to appear on the primary ballot; Kennealy's establishment backing could help him clear this hurdle, but a crowded field makes his path to 15% uncertain.
