## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does Djokovic's age compare to the oldest French Open champions in history?

The oldest French Open men's champion in the Open Era was Rafael Nadal at 36 in 2022. At 39, Djokovic would shatter this record by nearly three years, entering unprecedented territory for clay court endurance.

### What would need to happen for these odds to increase significantly before the tournament?

Strong performances at the 2025 French Open or winning the 2026 Australian Open would boost his odds considerably, as would sustained injuries to Alcaraz or Sinner during the 2025-2026 season.

### Why is clay particularly challenging for an aging Djokovic compared to other surfaces?

Clay court points last longer and require more grinding rallies and lateral movement than hard courts, placing greater demands on stamina and joint health - precisely the attributes that decline most with age.
