Redistricting after 2020 actually made the seat safer for Republicans. Unless Democrats recruit an exceptionally strong candidate and national conditions shift dramatically leftward by 2026, the structural advantage appears decisive. If national midterm dynamics favor Democrats or if the Republican nominee proves weak or embroiled in scandal, the 91.5% odds could compress sharply.
