Critical factors to monitor include announcement of token economics and vesting schedules, which will determine realistic initial circulation and selling pressure. Exchange listing confirmations, particularly from top-tier platforms, historically add 30-50% premiums to launch valuations. Watch for testnet metrics showing actual compute utilization versus theoretical capacity, as projects with demonstrable product-market fit command higher valuations. Any venture round disclosures revealing pre-launch valuations would provide crucial anchoring points for expectations.
