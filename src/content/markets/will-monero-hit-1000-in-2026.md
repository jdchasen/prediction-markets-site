## Frequently Asked Questions

### What makes Monero different from other privacy features on cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin or Zcash?

Monero enforces privacy by default on every transaction, automatically obscuring sender, receiver, and amounts, while Bitcoin transactions are fully transparent and Zcash privacy is optional (with less than 10% of transactions using shielded addresses). This makes Monero the only major cryptocurrency offering consistent fungibility where all coins are interchangeable.

### Which specific regulatory actions pose the biggest threat to Monero reaching $1000?

A U.S. Treasury designation of Monero as a "primary money laundering concern" under Section 311 of the Patriot Act or EU member states implementing outright bans under national security provisions would likely cut off most exchange access and eliminate realistic paths to mainstream adoption needed for 4x price appreciation.

### Could institutional adoption realistically drive Monero to $1000 despite its privacy features?

Institutional participation remains highly unlikely given compliance requirements and KYC obligations that conflict with Monero's anonymity, meaning any price surge would need to come from retail users, gray-market adoption, or geopolitical crisis scenarios rather than traditional investment channels that powered other cryptocurrencies to similar valuations.
