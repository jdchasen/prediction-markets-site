## Frequently Asked Questions

### What would gold's price trajectory need to look like to realistically reach $5,700 by June 2026?

Gold would need to sustain a ~6% quarterly rally for 6 consecutive quarters with minimal pullbacks, breaking through $3,000-$3,500 resistance zones decisively. This requires either a structural collapse in real yields or a major currency crisis—scenarios the market currently prices at ~7% probability.

### How does the dollar's strength affect this market's odds?

A strong dollar (DXY above 105) directly suppresses gold demand and makes the $5,700 target harder to reach; conversely, if DXY breaks below 100 and holds, it could provide meaningful tailwinds that push odds toward 15-20%.

### What Fed action would most significantly shift these odds?

An emergency rate cut cycle triggered by financial instability or recession would be the primary game-changer; if the Fed cuts below 2% by Q4 2025, odds could double or triple as real yields turn deeply negative.
