## Market Analysis

The bull case rests on Peru's volatility and anti-establishment sentiment that has elected outsiders in recent cycles—Pedro Castillo won in 2021 despite polling in single digits months before the election. Pérez Tello's legal background and technocratic credentials could position her as a "serious outsider" if the leading candidates implode through corruption scandals, which remains likely given Peru's institutional crisis. If she secures backing from a major political movement or builds a coalition among center-right factions frustrated with traditional parties, her odds could shift dramatically. The first-round vote scheduled for April 2026 uses a runoff system, meaning she would only need to place in the top two to have a path forward.

The bear case is overwhelming: Pérez Tello has shown no indication of building a campaign infrastructure, lacks name recognition outside Lima's political circles, and has no clear electoral base in a country where regionalism and populism dominate. Peru's presidential field typically features 15-20 candidates, with vote share fragmenting among anti-system figures, regional strongmen, and scandal-plagued veterans. Recent polling for the 2026 race shows figures like Keiko Fujimori, George Forsyth, and Rafael López Aliaga commanding double-digit support, while Pérez Tello doesn't register. Her association with the Kuczynski administration—which ended in his resignation amid corruption allegations—represents a liability rather than an asset.

Traders should monitor whether Pérez Tello formally declares candidacy and which party lists her as a candidate, with registration deadlines typically falling in December 2025. Watch for polling by Ipsos Peru and IEP (Instituto de Estudios Peruanos) starting in mid-2025 that will show the evolving field. The congressional elections held concurrently with the presidential race could reshape the landscape, and any major corruption revelations about frontrunners in the next 12 months would significantly alter the probability distribution across all candidates.
