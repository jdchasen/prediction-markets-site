## Market Analysis

The bear case, which traders are heavily backing, centers on the PPP's catastrophic position following Yoon's brief martial law declaration in December 2024 and subsequent impeachment. Recent polling shows the main opposition Democratic Party leading by 20-30 percentage points nationally, with the PPP struggling to distance itself from Yoon's actions while maintaining party unity. Local elections typically serve as referendums on the ruling party, and the June 2026 timing gives limited opportunity for rehabilitation. The party faces internal fractures between pro-Yoon loyalists and reformists, potentially splitting their vote or depressing turnout among conservative voters disillusioned by the chaos.

The bull case requires a dramatic reversal driven by several factors: successful economic reforms under an interim PPP leadership that restores public trust, major scandals engulfing the Democratic Party before mid-2025, or prosecution outcomes that somehow vindicate Yoon's actions or implicate opposition figures in parallel controversies. The Constitutional Court's ruling on Yoon's impeachment (expected by mid-2025) could theoretically provide clarity that allows the PPP to rebuild, particularly if the court's decision is less damaging than anticipated. Additionally, if the DP overreaches with aggressive policies after consolidating power, a voter backlash could materialize by early 2026.

Key catalysts to monitor include the Constitutional Court's final impeachment verdict, PPP leadership elections likely in Q1 2025, National Assembly by-elections that will test party strength, and polling trends through late 2025 that will show whether the opposition's dominance is structural or temporary. Presidential elections must be held within 60 days of impeachment confirmation, making that vote another critical indicator of conservative viability before the local elections. The party's performance in any special elections during 2025 will provide the earliest concrete signal of whether recovery is even plausible.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What specific local positions are being contested in the 2026 elections?

The elections will determine mayors and governors for all metropolitan cities and provinces, plus district-level positions and local council seats across South Korea. These positions control significant budgets and policy implementation at the regional level.

### Could the PPP win some regions even if they lose nationally?

Traditional conservative strongholds like Daegu and North Gyeongsang Province could remain competitive, but the market asks if PPP wins the overall elections (most seats/votes nationally), not individual races. Even regional victories wouldn't satisfy the winning condition if the DP dominates nationally.

### How does South Korea's impeachment process timeline affect the PPP's campaign preparation?

The Constitutional Court has up to 180 days to rule, meaning a decision could come as late as June 2025, just one year before local elections. This compressed timeframe leaves minimal opportunity for a new PPP leadership to rebrand and campaign effectively.
