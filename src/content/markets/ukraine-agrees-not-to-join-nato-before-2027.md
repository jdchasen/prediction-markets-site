The bear case points to Ukraine's ironclad constitutional commitment and domestic political consensus. Any Ukrainian leader agreeing to permanent or long-term NATO exclusion would face severe domestic backlash and potential political collapse.

Key catalysts include the June 2025 NATO summit, any major battlefield developments that shift leverage, and potential Trump administration peace proposals in early 2025. Ukraine's 2026 elections could theoretically produce leadership more amenable to compromise, though candidates supporting NATO renunciation currently lack viability. Traders should monitor whether Western aid packages include conditions related to NATO aspirations and whether Russia formally demands NATO exclusion as a precondition for negotiations. The market's low probability suggests traders see Ukraine's NATO position as fundamentally locked despite the war's uncertainties.
