Historical precedent shows drivers rarely win championships within their first two seasons at a new top team, and the 2026 regulation changes create uncertainty that typically favors experienced drivers who can develop cars effectively.

Traders should monitor Red Bull's driver decisions during the 2025 season, particularly around the summer break when team performance typically triggers seat evaluations.
