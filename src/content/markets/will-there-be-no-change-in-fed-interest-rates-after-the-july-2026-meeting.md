## Market Analysis

If CPI prints through 2025 consistently show month-over-month gains of 0.2% or less, and NFP reports demonstrate labor market stability without wage spiral pressures, the Fed would likely pause any adjustment campaign well before July 2026. Markets are pricing in most potential cuts happening in 2024-2025, leaving the July 2026 meeting as a maintenance hold. The current 92.5% probability suggests traders see the Fed's normalization cycle concluding at least six months before this meeting date.

The 7.5% probability of a change essentially prices in tail risk scenarios.
