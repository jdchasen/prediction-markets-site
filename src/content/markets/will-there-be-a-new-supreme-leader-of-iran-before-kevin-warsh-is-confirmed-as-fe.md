Conversely, if Warsh's Senate hearings occur in February-March 2025 with a floor vote by April-May, confirmation odds shift dramatically. Traders should monitor health reports from Iranian state media, any shifts in Trump's Fed nominee timing, and Senate Republican appetite for expedited confirmation processes.
