## Frequently Asked Questions

### What specific Treasury yield level would the market need to see sustained to make 4.8% still plausible?

Yields would need to consistently trade above 4.5% through mid-2025 and show no signs of retreating, requiring either a sustained inflation reacceleration or a policy error from the Fed.

### Could unexpected fiscal tightening (like a sudden spending cut) actually help this market's odds by raising term premiums?

Yes—if fiscal contraction signals recession risk, that could paradoxically push 10-year yields down despite inflation concerns, as flight-to-safety dynamics typically dominate during growth scares.

### If 2-year yields stay anchored near 4.0% through March 2025, is 4.8% on the 10-year still viable?

Highly unlikely—a flat 2-10 curve or inverted curve strongly suggests the market expects Fed cuts, making a 4.8% 10-year extremely difficult without a major shock to the 2-year expectations anchor.
