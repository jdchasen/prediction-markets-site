## Frequently Asked Questions

### What counts as a "transit" in this market definition—does a ship partially entering the Strait count, or must it fully pass through?

Market specifications typically require a complete passage from entry to exit in the Gulf of Oman side, counted by major shipping databases like Lloyd's List or Refinitiv; partial transits do not count.

### How sensitive is this market to Houthi attacks versus direct Iran-U.S. military action?

Houthi attacks on ships raise insurance costs and cause some route avoidance but rarely stop 20+ daily transits entirely; a direct U.S.-Iran naval clash or confirmed Iranian blockade action would be far more disruptive to hitting the threshold.

### Could OPEC's March meeting announcement of production cuts actually increase transit numbers as traders front-run price expectations?

Yes—paradoxically, a dovish OPEC signal in March could boost crude exports and thus tanker transits, potentially helping the YES side; conversely, a production cut announcement might reduce demand and lower transit volumes.
