The bull case for YES (lower inflation) rests on the assumption that the Fed's aggressive rate hiking from 2022-2023 continues exerting disinflationary pressure through 2026, wage growth moderates as labor market slack widens, and energy prices remain contained. ### What specific CPI print would likely move this market substantially higher?

### Why are odds so low at 1.9% despite the Fed potentially cutting rates in 2025?

### If the Fed cuts rates aggressively in 2025, wouldn't that make YES more likely?
