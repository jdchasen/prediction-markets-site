## Frequently Asked Questions

### Has the U.S. Embassy in Beirut been evacuated before, and what triggered it?

Yes, the embassy was evacuated in 2006 during the Israel-Hezbollah war, with staff airlifted out. A similar military escalation would be the primary mechanism for triggering evacuation under current market conditions.

### What specific security incidents at the embassy compound could force evacuation?

Direct rocket or drone strikes causing American casualties, penetration of the compound perimeter by hostile actors, or inability of Lebanese security forces to maintain basic access control would likely trigger immediate evacuation orders.

### If Lebanon's economic crisis worsens significantly, would that alone cause evacuation?

Unlikely—economic collapse alone doesn't typically trigger embassy evacuation unless it creates a complete security vacuum or enables non-state armed groups to take over Beirut. The bear case assumes Lebanon's political structure, however weak, prevents total state breakdown through 2026.
