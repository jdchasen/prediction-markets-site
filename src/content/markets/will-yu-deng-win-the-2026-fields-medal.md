## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does the Fields Medal selection committee actually choose winners, and when do they make their decision?

The committee of 8-10 mathematicians appointed by the IMU Executive Committee deliberates confidentially for 12-18 months before the Congress, typically finalizing selections 3-6 months prior to the July announcement. No official information leaks before the ceremony, making this market purely speculative until the actual reveal.

### What mathematical achievements would Yu Deng need to have accomplished to be considered a serious Fields Medal contender?

A Fields Medal typically requires either solving a long-standing major problem, introducing revolutionary new techniques that open entire research areas, or making multiple fundamental contributions that reshape a field. Work must demonstrate exceptional depth and originality beyond standard strong publications.

### Why is this market categorized under politics rather than science or academics?

This appears to be a miscategorization, as the Fields Medal is a purely academic honor decided by mathematical merit without political considerations, though institutional politics within mathematics academia can influence perceptions of importance and impact.
