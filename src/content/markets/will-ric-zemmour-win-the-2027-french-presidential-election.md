## Market Analysis

The bull case hinges on a dramatic implosion of the National Rally between now and 2027, potentially through Le Pen's legal troubles—her embezzlement trial verdict could result in a ban from holding office, expected before 2027. If RN fractures or Le Pen becomes ineligible, Zemmour's Reconquête party could absorb disaffected far-right voters. His media savvy and ideological clarity on immigration and national identity could also capitalize on any major terrorist attack or migration crisis that pushes voters toward harder-line positions. France's presidential first round on April 10, 2027, allows multiple candidates, and a splintered field could theoretically propel him to the May runoff if establishment parties collapse further.

The bear case is overwhelming: Zemmour's 2022 campaign peaked early at 17% in polls before collapsing to 7% in the actual vote, demonstrating his ceiling with voters. His party won zero seats in the 2022 legislative elections and has failed to build grassroots infrastructure. National Rally's June 2024 European Parliament success and strong performance in the July 2024 snap legislative elections show Le Pen has successfully absorbed the far-right electorate. Zemmour's provocative statements have resulted in multiple legal convictions for hate speech, limiting his appeal beyond hardcore supporters. His advanced age (he'll be 69 in 2027) and lack of any governing experience make him unpalatable to centrist voters needed in a runoff.

Key catalysts include Le Pen's legal verdict timeline (likely by spring 2025), the 2026 regional elections that could show Reconquête's organizational strength, and any major security incidents that historically boost far-right polling. Traders should monitor whether Zemmour maintains his MEP seat visibility, whether RN experiences internal splits over Jordan Bardella's leadership, and polling for the first round throughout 2026. Absent a Le Pen disqualification or RN collapse, the current odds rationally reflect Zemmour's position as a spent political force.

### What happens to this market if Marine Le Pen is banned from running due to her embezzlement conviction?

### Could Zemmour withdraw and endorse another candidate before the election?

Yes, and this represents a realistic scenario—if his polling remains in single digits by early 2027, he might endorse Le Pen or another right-wing candidate in exchange for political concessions. The market would resolve NO if he doesn't personally run and win.

### How does France's two-round system affect Zemmour's actual path to victory?

He would need roughly 15-20% in the first round on April 10, 2027 to reach the runoff, then win an absolute majority two weeks later on April 24—requiring him to attract centrist voters who have consistently rejected his hardline positions in polling matchups.
