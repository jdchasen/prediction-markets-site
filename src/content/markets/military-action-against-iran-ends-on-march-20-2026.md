The window between November 2025 and March 2026 could see heightened risk if Iran appears close to breakout capability.

Critical monitoring points include quarterly IAEA reports on Iran's uranium enrichment levels, any Israeli strikes on Iranian nuclear facilities (which could trigger retaliation spirals), and U.S. military deployments to the Gulf region. Regional proxy activity in Iraq, Yemen, and the Strait of Hormuz serves as a leading indicator of Tehran's risk tolerance.
