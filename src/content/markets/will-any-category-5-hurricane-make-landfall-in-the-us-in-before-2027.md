## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does the Saffir-Simpson scale measurement work for resolution—does the hurricane need to be Category 5 at peak intensity, or specifically at landfall?

Resolution requires Category 5 classification (sustained winds 157+ mph) at the time of actual landfall, not just peak intensity offshore; storms often weaken during approach, which is why Category 5 landfalls are rarer than Category 5 formation.

### Has the frequency of Category 5 hurricanes increased due to warming, and what does climate data suggest about baseline rates?

Long-term records show no clear increasing trend in Category 5 frequency (still roughly 1 per 15-20 years in US landfalls), though some research suggests warmer oceans may increase intensity potential; the 15.5% odds imply traders are pricing elevated risk relative to historical norms.
