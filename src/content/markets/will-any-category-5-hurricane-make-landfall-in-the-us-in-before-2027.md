## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do forecasters distinguish between Category 5 storms that form versus those making landfall, and why does this market require both?

The Saffir-Simpson scale measures peak winds anywhere during a storm's lifetime, but landfall requires the hurricane to cross the US coastline while still at Category 5 strength; many weaken before reaching land or track offshore entirely, which is why landfall is measurably rarer than formation.

### What specific data releases would most directly signal changing odds for this market before the 2025 season begins?

The National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) releases its official Atlantic hurricane season forecast in August 2025, including predicted number of hurricanes and major hurricanes; significantly above-normal calls would immediately justify higher YES odds.

### If a Category 5 hurricane forms in the Atlantic in November 2026 but doesn't make landfall until January 2027, does this market resolve YES?

No—the market expires December 31, 2026, so only landfalls occurring before that cutoff count; a storm forming before expiry but making landfall after the deadline would not resolve the market as YES.
