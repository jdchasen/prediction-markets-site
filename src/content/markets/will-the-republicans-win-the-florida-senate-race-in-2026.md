If Republicans nominate a strong candidate with executive experience (likely from the gubernatorial ranks or a well-funded primary winner), they inherit structural advantages in a state where registered Republicans outnumber Democrats by roughly 300,000 voters. The bear case centers on midterm fundamentals and potential candidate weakness. Historically, the party holding the White House loses Senate seats in midterms, and if Trump's second term generates significant unpopularity by 2026, Florida could prove more competitive than current odds suggest.
