### Why would Milei push for dollarization if Congress almost certainly won't pass it?

Dollarization serves as a political signaling device to radical free-market voters and allows Milei to blame Congress for inflation persistence, while alternative measures like expanding dollar deposits can deliver partial benefits without requiring legislative consensus.

### Could Argentina dollarize without formal legal adoption, and would that resolve this market?

No—the contract specifies official dollarization by law, not de facto dollar circulation. Argentina already permits dollar deposits and transactions; informal dollarization wouldn't satisfy the contract terms and would require explicit legislative action to settle YES.

### What would a severe peso crisis in 2025 actually trigger instead of dollarization?

Emergency central bank measures like capital controls, deposit freezes, or currency board arrangements (similar to 2001) are historically more likely in Argentina's playbook than constitutional reforms, as they require only executive action rather than supermajority congressional support.
