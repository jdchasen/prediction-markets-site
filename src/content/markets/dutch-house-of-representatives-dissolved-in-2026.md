### How do Dutch dissolution mechanics affect this market's resolution?

Under Dutch law, the government can dissolve parliament unilaterally or via constructive vote of no confidence; either path requires active political decision-making, meaning dysfunction alone doesn't guarantee dissolution—parties must choose to call elections.
