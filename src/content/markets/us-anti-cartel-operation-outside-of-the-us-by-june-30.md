## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does "outside the U.S." include operations where U.S. companies are defendants or only foreign-defendant cases?

The resolution criteria typically require that the operation itself occurs in foreign territory or targets primarily foreign-based defendants; U.S. companies tried in American courts don't qualify even if the cartel operated internationally.

### Which industries are most likely to trigger an enforcement action by June 2026?

Pharmaceuticals, chemicals, semiconductors, and financial services are statistically the most active cartel sectors with established international coordination mechanisms that make coordinated raids feasible.

### How much does a change in U.S. administration affect the probability of this outcome?

Significantly—new administrations typically deprioritize international enforcement during their first 6-9 months while establishing domestic priorities, which compresses the operational window considerably for a June 2026 deadline.
