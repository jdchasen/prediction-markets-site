Key catalysts include any formal IPO filing announcement (typically 6-12 months before going public), OpenAI's restructuring away from nonprofit control expected in 2024-2025, and quarterly revenue disclosures that signal growth sustainability. GPT-5's release timeline and capabilities will materially impact valuation expectations, as will Microsoft's quarterly earnings calls discussing Azure OpenAI service adoption. Traders should monitor competitor product launches, particularly Google's Gemini updates and any major enterprise defections, while watching for regulatory developments around AI safety that could complicate OpenAI's governance transition.
