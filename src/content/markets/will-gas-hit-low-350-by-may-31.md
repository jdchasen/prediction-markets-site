## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the historical relationship between crude oil prices and $3.50 retail gasoline?

Retail gas prices typically reach $3.50 when WTI crude is in the $55-65 range; at current $70+ levels, reaching that threshold requires a 10-15% crude decline plus refining margin compression or demand destruction.

### Could seasonal summer driving demand in May 2026 prevent this outcome regardless of crude levels?

Yes—May is peak driving season with typical seasonal strength, which historically adds $0.15-0.30 to national averages and acts as a significant headwind to reaching the low end of the forecast range.

### What legislative or policy changes between now and May 2026 could most impact this market?

Tariff policy on energy goods, strategic petroleum reserve release decisions by the incoming administration (January 2025 onward), and any new Iran sanctions would be the primary policy levers affecting crude supply and thus retail prices.
